## License boundary

The book's copyright page states that the main copyrightable work is licensed under CC BY 4.0, while third-party text, graphics, images, tables, charts, and graphs remain with their respective owners and are not covered automatically.

Policy for RU v2:

- project-generated biblioteka-vsp chapter visuals: **PERMITTED**
- source visual with explicit third-party provenance/citation: **BLOCKED unless separate permission/license is verified**
- source visual derived from an external model/framework with unclear graphic ownership: **REVIEW_REQUIRED**
- source visual that is clearly part of the book author's own DAIR model and has no external attribution in the canonical text: **BOOK_NATIVE_CANDIDATE**, but original raster/vector asset is not inserted until source ownership is verified at asset level
