## Acceptance checks

A DAIR implementation is audit-ready when:
- each activity generates retrievable evidence;
- decision rationale is recorded;
- iterations are explainable;
- Decision Maker participation is visible;
- incident docs can be mapped to CSF functions;
- debrief improvements are tracked to implementation.
