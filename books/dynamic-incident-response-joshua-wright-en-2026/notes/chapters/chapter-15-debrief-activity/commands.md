# Команды и operational artifacts Chapter 15

Chapter 15 не содержит shell/CLI-команд.

Это governance, documentation и continuous-improvement chapter.

## Source-supported artifacts

### Incident closure record

Should capture:
- closure decision;
- decision maker;
- residual risk;
- temporary asset disposition;
- evidence retention;
- monitoring status.

### Unified incident record

Consolidate:
- incident ticket;
- chat;
- notes;
- timeline;
- evidence references;
- technical decisions;
- stakeholder decisions.

### Incident metrics

Capture:
- MTTD;
- MTTR;
- scope;
- impact;
- responder hours;
- third-party cost;
- total lifecycle.

### After-Action Review

Core questions:
- expected?
- actual?
- why?
- improve?
- worked?
- why worked?

### Recommendation tracker

Minimum fields:
- recommendation;
- rationale;
- priority;
- owner;
- deadline;
- status;
- dependencies;
- follow-up date.

### Executive lever

Fields:
- Opportunity
- Benefit
- Cost
- Time
- Resources
- Compliance
- Protections

## Verification / acceptance

Debrief is not complete until:

- authoritative incident record exists;
- required evidence is retained;
- temporary assets have disposition;
- metrics captured;
- AAR completed;
- recommendations have owners;
- due dates assigned;
- follow-up mechanism exists.
