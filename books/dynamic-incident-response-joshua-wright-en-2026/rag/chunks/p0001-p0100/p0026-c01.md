PART 1: ELEMENTS OF INCIDENT RESPONSE
In this section we’ll start with a case study of an incident affecting a fictitious organization. We’ll also
introduce the key concepts and elements for this book, examine the changing demands on incident
response teams, and look at existing incident response processes and models.
