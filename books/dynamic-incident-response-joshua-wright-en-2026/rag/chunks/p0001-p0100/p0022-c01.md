STATEMENT ON AI USE
I believe we have a right to know when the content we are consuming is generated by AI. Further, I think it’s
reasonable for readers to ask if the book they are thinking about reading is worth their investment versus
being a shallow piece of content generated by AI tools. In this section, I disclose how I used AI tools in the
research, coding, image creation, and writing of this book.

Research
At several points, I used AI tools, including Claude, Claude Code, and ChatGPT, to conduct research for this
book. Many of the stories and experiences came from my own work, but I also used AI tools to research
topics and gather supportive or contradictory information to ensure I showcased a broader perspective
than just my own experiences.

   I’m interested in the tradeoffs for the use of commercial vs. open source cybersecurity tools, specifically to “   identify the factors that organizations should consider when deciding when to use one or the other. What
   works best for organizations of different sizes or employee skill levels? What factors should companies
   consider? Is there a decision tree that companies should consider when thinking about pursuing a
   commercial versus an open source cybersecurity tool?
