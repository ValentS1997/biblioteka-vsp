offers a way of thinking that survives contact with real incidents.

— Rob T. Lee, Chief AI Officer and Chief of Research, SANS Institute

I’ve seen too many breach reports where defenders detected attackers early but scoped poorly, eradicated“incompletely, or prioritized prevention without detection. Dynamic Incident Response is a clear, practical

— Jeff McJunkin, Penetration Tester and SANS Author
