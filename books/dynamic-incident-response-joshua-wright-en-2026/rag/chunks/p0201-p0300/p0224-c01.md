Scope Activity11

In the DAIR model, we introduce another activity not specifically called out in other popular incident
response models: scope. This step bridges the gap between  initial incident verification and effective
containment by determining the extent of the compromise across the organization. Without proper
scoping, incident response teams risk addressing only the visible symptoms while leaving significant
portions of the attack undetected and unaddressed.
