9  Verify and Triage    Activities

In the DAIR model, we introduce an activity that is not specifically called out in other popular incident
response models: verify and triage. This step is often implied in the detection phase of PICERL or NIST SP
800-61 (Computer Security Incident Handling Guide), but we believe it is important to explicitly call it out.
