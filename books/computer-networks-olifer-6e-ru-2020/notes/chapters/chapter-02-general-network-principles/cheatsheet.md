Scope: PDF 41-74. Source: RAG only.

## Symptom / problem framing
The chapter explains the conceptual tasks required for computers to communicate and share resources.

## Baseline / context
Key areas: interfaces, protocols, software services, physical transmission, topology, addressing and switching.

## Read-only checks
- Distinguish interface from protocol.
- Distinguish channel capacity from measured throughput.
- Distinguish route selection from per-node forwarding.
- Distinguish multiplexing from a shared medium.

## Interpretation
A network is built from coordinated physical, logical and software mechanisms. As node count grows, topology, addressing and transit communication become separate design problems.
