# Субъективный список полезных книг — список автора

Source: EPUB spine `14`, `OPS/ch1-13.xhtml`, RAG `mama-ya-timlid-ru:e0014:c01–c02`.

This is **Marina Pereskokova's subjective reading list from the source**. It is not an independent ranking or recommendation by this repository.

| # | Book | Why the author says it was useful |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Том Демарко, Тимоти Листер — **«Человеческий фактор: Успешные проекты и команды»** | Helped validate the idea that human problems and relationships are a central part of management rather than distracting «nannying». |
| 2 | Даниэль Канеман — **«Думай медленно… решай быстро»** | Useful for understanding cognitive traps, sunk-cost behavior, risk perception and decision-making; the author especially points to value for product managers. |
| 3 | Стивен Кови — **«7 навыков высокоэффективных людей»** | The author highlights living/working consistently with values and goals, integrity in relationships and useful principles of delegation. |
| 4 | Юлия Гиппенрейтер — **«Общаться с ребенком. Как?»** | The author draws transferable lessons about communication, reaction to mistakes and teaching; explicitly notes that subordinates are not children. |
| 5 | Эрих Фромм — **«Искусство любить»** | Helped the author think about how different people can be in motivation, expectations and relationships. |
| 6 | Рэй Далио — **«Принципы: Жизнь и работа»** | Included despite not yet passing the author's own «test of time» because of the idea that people are non-universal and teams should be composed around individual strengths and weaknesses. |
| 7 | Нассим Талеб — **«Черный лебедь: Под знаком непредсказуемости»** | Helped the author remain calmer when making decisions under deep uncertainty and when later judging decisions after unpredictable consequences become known. |
| 8 | Игорь Рызов — **«Переговоры с монстрами»** | Described as a practical guide for negotiation with someone in a clearly stronger position; useful to the author in cross-department negotiations. |
| 9 | Эрин Мейер — **«Карта культурных различий»** | Useful for understanding differences in how people from different cultures think, lead and interact, and for seeing one's own norms from the outside. |
