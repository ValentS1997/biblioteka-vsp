## Quantitative source examples

- Source suggests roughly **10 new models per analyst** as a practical ratio, explicitly depending on model complexity (`e0045:c02–c03`).
- Models should be continuously monitored relative to risk tolerance.
- Audit should request evidence for **all** methods, not only mathematically complex ones.

These are source recommendations, not executable standards.
