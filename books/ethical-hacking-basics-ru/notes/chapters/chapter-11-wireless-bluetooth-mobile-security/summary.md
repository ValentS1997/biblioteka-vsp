# Авторское итоговое резюме главы 11

**Источник:** `11.2 Bluetooth - сканирование, атаки. Мобильные устройства`, **Шаг 7**, начинается на PDF p. **322** и продолжается на p. **323**; chunks `ethical-hacking-basics-ru:p0322:c01`, `p0322:c02`, `p0323:c01`.

## 1. Wi‑Fi topologies and authentication

Source summary возвращается к infrastructure/ad-hoc wireless networks, station/AP terminology and authentication. Open authentication means association without a network password, while protected networks add authentication/encryption layers.

## 2. WEP / WPA / WPA2 / WPA3

Source последовательно описывает WEP, WPA, WPA2 и WPA3 как развитие wireless security. В summary подчёркиваются weakness WEP, WPA as transitional improvement, WPA2 as long-term replacement, Personal/Enterprise variants, and WPA3 improvements.

## 3. Wireless capture and attacks

Summary перечисляет:
- monitor-mode capture and Wireshark;
- collection/analysis of wireless frames;
- deauthentication;
- evil-twin scenarios;
- key-reinstallation concept.

Artifacts retain these as threat/evidence concepts; disruptive commands are not reproduced.

## 4. Bluetooth attack classes

Source names bluejacking, bluesnarfing and bluebugging and notes that some Bluetooth attacks may be old/less applicable to modern devices, while legacy devices can remain exposed.

## 5. Mobile devices

The source closes with mobile-platform threats: third-party app stores, malware/spyware, phishing/smishing, wireless-data leakage and programming/crypto flaws.

## Defensive interpretation

The chapter’s practical defensive themes are: strong modern wireless authentication/encryption, network segmentation, controlled BYOD access, passive wireless monitoring, avoidance of untrusted apps/networks, and validation of Bluetooth/mobile exposure on current devices.
