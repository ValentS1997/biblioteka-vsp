# Review questions — Appendix A

**Provenance:** `p0717:c01`–`p0719:c01`.

1. Convert 1984, 4000 and 8192 to binary.
2. Convert `1001101001₂` to decimal, octal and hexadecimal.
3. Which of BED, CAB, DEAD, DECADE, ACCEDED, BAG, DAD are valid hex numbers?
4. Express decimal 100 in bases 2 through 9.
5. How many positive integers fit in k radix-r digits?
6. Binary counting on 10 fingers / 20 fingers; 20-bit two's-complement count.
7. Four 8-bit two's-complement calculations.
8. Repeat them in ones' complement.
9. For five 3-bit two's-complement sums: sign, zero, overflow.
10. Encode 6, -2, 100, -14, -1, 0 in 3-digit nine's complement.
11. State nine's-complement addition rule and calculate four sums.
12. State ten's-complement addition rule.
13. Build radix-3 multiplication table.
14. Multiply binary `0111 × 0011`.
15. Program: signed decimal ASCII input → octal, hex and two's-complement binary.
16. Program: add two 32-character two's-complement binary strings.
